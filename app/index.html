🗺 Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough, 0 online trophies

Built from the official trophy descriptions and the PowerPyx roadmap (difficulty 4/10, ~200 hours, no mission replay, no difficulty settings, cheats disable trophies and saving). There are no online trophies — everything is single-player and offline.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–2 (first ~15 hours). Finish the opening mission Gone Fishing, then Chapter 1 and Chapter 2. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1 — it is the game's only missable trophy step (see Missables). Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter: there is no mission replay.
  2. Stage 2 — Chapters 3–4 + side strands. Finish the remaining chapter and mission trophies (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory), the side-character finals (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and 8 stranger strands. Do time-sensitive stranger missions the moment they appear. Robberies open up here: bank vaults, armored trucks, shops, jewelry stores.
  3. Stage 3 — The final mission, then free-roam. Complete the final mission for the gold, then keep playing in free-roam. Nothing toward 100% is missable after the story.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & activities cleanup. Meditation spots, 3 collectible card sets, 100 WAiNK It locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all of Leonida explored, all garages bought; then the activity grind: 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, drone races at every location, 20 different race wins, 50+ challenges, fishing vs Cal, arcade, bowling, hunting for Bret.
  5. Stage 5 — 100% Completion & the platinum. Open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion and finish the checklist — PowerPyx reports it is roughly 60% of true completion and needs only 70 of 80 challenges, but shooting ranges, drone races, robberies, underwater discoveries and Bret's quests need everything. A Long Road (gold) is the hardest trophy; the platinum Vice Got a Price! pops right after it.

⚠ Missable trophies

1 trophy is flagged missable in this guide — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida). PowerPyx flags it as the only potentially missable trophy: the first meditation, Lucia on the balcony of Jason's house in Chapter 1 (after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning and accept), disappears after Chapter 1, and the game has no mission replay. Do it before finishing Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

Watch-list (not missable trophies, but timed content): stranger strands can include time-sensitive optional missions — Output Lag reports a Chapter 3 street race with an hourglass icon. Optional missions disappearing does not block any trophy, per PowerPyx, because there are far more than the 8 strands Strangers & Freaks needs. No online trophies exist, so nothing is locked behind multiplayer.
